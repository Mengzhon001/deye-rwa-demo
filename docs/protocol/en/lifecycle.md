# From application to issuance

Applications and document reviews start in the backend. Onchain business actions begin when the intermediary mints the station credential. Household custody approval and intermediary admission follow as separate transactions. A single button does not sign for three roles.

## Application and agreement

The household selects a station and applies to sell revenue rights. The intermediary may request supplements, reject or approve the documents. Document approval is not offer approval. The intermediary prepares an evidence-based offer; a trustee reviews it; the household accepts the price and signs an agreement bound to the station, share, term, chain and product.

Material changes in tariffs, costs or baseline require reassessment and the applicable approvals and acceptance. Old prices cannot simply be reused to force issuance.

## Three-step admission

<div class="diagram"><span>Intermediary mints NFT to owner</span> → <span>Owner authorizes vault custody</span> → <span>Intermediary admits rights</span></div>

The NFT and agreement hash identify the station and its terms. Admission establishes custody and the purchase-price obligation; it does not mint investor shares. Equipment control and title do not transfer.

## Valuation and publication

The intermediary prepares and submits a pool valuation. A trustee approves it in a separate transaction. The intermediary then reviews the funding target, issuance conditions, projected net IRR and retained cash before publishing terms. Only ProductPublished makes the product visible in the hall.

Each current scenario contains one product. Published terms cannot be freely edited or replaced for existing orders without renewed acceptance. Different terms require the supported approval workflow or, for a demo, a new scenario.

## Fundraising versus share issuance

Publication opens subscriptions to eligible investors. Settlement pays households and mints DRS only after order-review, fundraising and acquisition-funding conditions are all satisfied. ProductSettled marks completed share issuance. Failed fundraising refunds escrow without issuing shares.
