# Protocol overview

Deye RWA pools a defined share of a solar station's household electricity payments and export revenue for a finite term. Onchain records connect households, intermediaries, investors and trustees. This is a local demonstration using test tokens, not an operational securities offering or real-money settlement service.

## What is the investment?

The household retains ownership of the solar and storage equipment. New agreements combine discounted household solar-electricity payments and eligible export receipts, assigning 100% of net station revenue by default for five years from settlement. Households default to paying 80% of the grid energy price for billable self-used solar electricity. The billing factor and assigned revenue share are independent terms. The signed agreement fixes the percentage, duration, deductible costs and collection responsibilities. Equipment residual value does not belong to the vault.

There are two layers of tokens. One station NFT identifies each station and evidences its agreed rights in custody. Investors receive DRS shares only after successful fundraising settlement. Shares participate in cash distributions and the remaining net asset value.

## What determines returns?

Returns depend on exported energy, tariffs, costs, collections, issuance pricing and exit conditions. Remaining finite rights can decline in value as cash is distributed. Read projected net IRR, cash distributions, remaining NAV and total return together; a distribution yield is not automatically an annualized return.

There is no guaranteed coupon or automatic repayment of original principal. Forecasts are estimates, and simulated receipts are demonstration data. Neither proves that a household has received an actual electricity payment.

## Three entry points

The homepage explains the protocol and shows curated current-scenario statistics. Documentation is public. Launch App opens role selection before connecting a wallet and requesting a sign-in signature.

The product hall includes only products whose fundraising has been published. Contract deployment is not product issuance. Drafts are excluded, fundraising and settled active products have distinct labels, and failed or closed products appear through the history filter.
